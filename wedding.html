About

Why Esatto?

'Esatto' is Italian for 'exactly'. We accept nothing less when fitting clients for custom clothing. We have only one goal: to help you find your esatto - the perfect fit.

Your Clothier

For over six years, I've had the pleasure of making men the best suits and shirts they've ever worn. Clients always ask how I got into making custom suits. It all started when my entire team at a college job got fired, 2 weeks before my wedding. Instead of waiting a few weeks to find a new job, my wife insisted I find a new job quickly. In some stroke of luck, a Garth's in Provo was hiring. The next year, I searched for my own factory and started fitting friends and family.

- Brandon Beck, Head Clothier
