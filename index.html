Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our custom suits, wedding tailoring, and the esatto fitting process.

How long does it take to get a suit made? ▾
After you place an order, everything will be ready in about 6 weeks.
What if I need a rush order? ▾
While our standard lead time is 6 weeks, we can rush an order in 2–3 weeks. Rush orders require an additional fee or purchase.
What do we do in a fitting? ▾
We'll talk about your vision for what you want to create and your style preferences. Then we'll go through our bespoke fitting process. After we have your measurements, we'll pick out fabrics and details for each piece we're making. The fitting ends with payment for the order. Fittings typically take 45–75 minutes depending on how many items you order.
Do you do alterations for existing garments? ▾
No, we only make new custom pieces. If you're looking for an alterations shop, we recommend and use Fanny Green.
Do you require a deposit? ▾
We require full payment up front for your order (we have to pay our factory when we place your order).
What colors do you have? ▾
We have over 1,000 fabric swatches to pick from. Whether you're after a dark red, green, or a shade of brown, we have it.
Can I come look at things or set up a consultation? ▾
We can schedule a fitting if you've decided that you're going to have us make you a suit. If you're still in research mode and want to feel things out, I'm happy to answer questions on a phone call, text, or email.
