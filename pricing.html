Our Fitting Process

Getting a custom suit at esatto is a streamlined, enjoyable experience designed to fit your schedule. Here's how it works:

1

Request a Fitting

Schedule a fitting at our Layton studio.

2

Fitting

We take over 30 body measurements and have you try on pants and several jackets to ensure all of our measurements are precisely accurate.

3

Styling

We pick your fabrics and design your suits, shirts, and shoes to match your needs.

4

Your Suit Is Made

Your pieces are crafted at our factory. Orders are ready in about 6 weeks.

5

Final Fitting

We schedule a fitting to try on your new clothing. If anything isn't right, we alter or remake until you're happy.
